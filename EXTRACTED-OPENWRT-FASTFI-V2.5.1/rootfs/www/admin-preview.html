How It Works
  • Offline licenses: No cloud. Seller uses Flasher-and-License to flash firmware and burn a signed KSK-… license onto the ESP32.
  • Detect / bind: Power the ESP on this router/AP, then use Detect ESP32 (auto if the ESP reports its license ID, or enter MAC + License ID). Insert Coin unlocks only when licensed and bound.
  • One live ESP per license: Each license runs on one MAC at a time. Detecting a new board under the same license drops the old MAC.
  • Grace (max 3): If an ESP dies, seller gives a replacement flasher bundle with the same sealed license. Flash the new ESP → Detect → continues. After 3 grace uses, buy a new license.
  • New license: Seller License Generator issues a fresh KSK-… after grace is exhausted.
  • Private WiFi: ESP may auto-join SSIDs containing CEBJ / your private SSID (default password often 12345678 — change it).
Need a new license or replacement flasher?

Contact your CEBJ.NET / KonekSik seller. They will either give you a replacement flasher bundle (same license, grace remaining) or a new KSK license from the License Generator.

Licenses are offline and bound to this router when Detected — not purchased from FastFi cloud.
